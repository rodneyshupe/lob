# Lob

Throw files, URLs and text between your devices (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux) through your own server. Items wait up to 24 hours for the receiving device, are end-to-end encrypted, and each person controls who may send to their devices.

Lob is a set of Flutter clients on top of a self-hosted, unmodified [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) server.
